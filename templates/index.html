Landslide Data Practicum

by Jack Turner

A landslide is the downslope movement of rock, soil, or debris caused when gravity overcomes the strength holding slope materials in place. They can be caused by heavy rainfall, earthquakes, volcanic activity, erosion, or human activity. This project aims to find areas where landfalls are most common, which have been the deadliest in human history, and how much news coverage these events receive.
